Scaling in

Does pyramiding rescue a losing system?

Turtle's specification adds a unit every half-N in favour, up to four, with the whole position's stop following the newest. Pyramiding is where trend following is supposed to earn — it scales into the trades that are working. For months this was the honest gap in the measurement: until it existed, "Turtle loses money" was not a claim about Turtle.

UnitsTradesWinExpectancy Totalt
Pyramiding is a multiplier, not a source of edge. It levers whatever edge the underlying rules have — compounding gains where that is positive, and losses where it is not. Scaling into a move that does not persist simply buys more of a losing trade at a worse price.

What it is deciding

Process quality, measured separately from whether the strategy works. A high decline rate is the design working, not a fault — most candidates should be declined.

Rubric behaviour

Which conditions are doing the rejecting

Distribution of scores per criterion, not just how often each vetoed. A criterion that always scores zero means the screen and the model disagree about what the rule is. One clustering in the middle means the model is hedging on a question that is really binary. A tally cannot tell those apart, and they need different fixes.
